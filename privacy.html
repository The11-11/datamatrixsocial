Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains what information Datamatrix ("we", "us") collects through this website, our Instagram account @datamatrix_applications, the apps we publish, and our Meta developer app, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Messages you send us by email or Instagram: your name, handle, contact details and what you write.
  • Instagram interactions with our account, such as comments, mentions and direct messages, which Meta sends to us so we can reply.
  • App usage events from our mobile apps, such as installs, launches and in-app purchases, measured with the Meta (Facebook) SDK. On iOS this only includes the advertising identifier if you allow tracking.

How we use it

  • To answer enquiries and deliver projects you ask us for.
  • To reply to comments and messages on Instagram.
  • To measure and improve our apps and our advertising on Meta platforms.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Meta Platforms receives data through Instagram and the Meta SDK, under Meta's Privacy Policy. We share nothing else with third parties unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and messages for as long as we need them to work with you, and delete them when they are no longer needed or when you ask.

Your choices

  • On iOS you can refuse app tracking when asked, or later under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time.

Deleting your data

Email hello@datamatrix.io with the subject "Data deletion request" and the Instagram handle or email address we know you by. We delete your data within 30 days and confirm by reply.
